📌 About Edgelink

Edgelink is a dynamic platform that bridges educators, organizers, and participants through real‑time interaction. Whether it’s live events, instant polls, word clouds, or gamified quizzes, we empower you to create engaging experiences. Our tools are designed to be intuitive, helping you focus on what matters most — connecting and learning together.
